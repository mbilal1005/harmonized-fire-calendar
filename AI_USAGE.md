# AI usage

| Tool / model | What it was used for | Human role |
|---|---|---|
| Claude (claude.ai, Claude Opus 5.5) | Planning and research: comparing challenges, data sources, method, customer framing, pitch and slides | Made the decisions and edited the text |
| Claude Code (Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.2) | Writing and debugging the data pipeline, validation and Streamlit app; Fable also reviewed the method for validity risks | Specified features and rules, checked results in the browser, chose which fixes to apply |

No AI generates text or numbers in the running app. All figures come from NASA FIRMS data, and the ML forecast test uses scikit-learn on our own aggregates.
